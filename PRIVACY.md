# Privacy Policy

Solina Launcher does not create accounts and does not send your personal data, usage statistics or
crash reports to Solina.

Features you choose to use may contact third-party services directly from your device, for example web
search providers, icon-pack apps, widgets, or installing updates from GitHub. Those services have their
own privacy policies.

The launcher needs some permissions only for the features that use them (for example usage access for
app suggestions, or accessibility service for lock-screen gestures). They are optional and can be
revoked in Android settings.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Solina-Projects/Solina-Launcher2/issues
