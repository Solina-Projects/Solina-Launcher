<p><i>Solina Launcher</i> is an open-source Android home screen focused on useful customization and a clean, flexible experience.</p><p>Built on Launcher3 and derived from the open-source Lawnchair codebase, Solina Launcher brings home-screen features such as adjustable icon size, labels, rows and columns, icon packs, search, and personalization options.</p><p>This project is under development. Features, availability, and compatibility may change between test builds. Back up your launcher settings before testing development versions.</p>
